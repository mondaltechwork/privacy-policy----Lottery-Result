Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Version: 1.0

1. Introduction & Overview

Lottery Result (referred to as "we", "our", or "the app") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy discloses our data collection, processing, and storage practices in connection with your use of the Lottery Result Android application.

We do not require user account registration, passwords, or personal login profiles to browse lottery results.

2. Device Permissions & Information Processed

The application requests and utilizes only the following minimum necessary Android permissions:

  • INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET): Allows the app to connect securely to public result servers to download public lottery draw announcements and authentic official PDF records.
  • ACCESS NETWORK STATE (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to detect active internet connectivity, allowing the app to serve locally stored results when offline.
  • ADVERTISING ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Required under Android 13+ for the Google Mobile Ads SDK to deliver compliant advertising.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device diagnostic information, and coarse network location to serve relevant ads.

You may manage or reset your advertising preferences at any time through your Android system settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

The app also utilizes Google ML Kit Text Recognition for on-device ticket scanning. Ticket image processing occurs entirely locally on your device hardware; images are never transmitted or stored on remote servers.

4. Local Storage & Data Retention

The app stores public lottery draw records and queries on local device storage to enable instant offline access without re-downloading previously viewed results.

We do not collect or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, banking details, or exact GPS locations on our servers.

Users can manage or clear locally stored application data at any time through standard Android system settings (Settings > Apps > Lottery Result > Storage).

5. Children's Privacy

Lottery Result is intended strictly for users who have attained the legal age for lottery reference in their jurisdiction (18+). The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
